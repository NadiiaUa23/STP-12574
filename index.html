About the Game

Unlike most puzzle games where you're constantly swapping tiles around, Bee Brilliant Blast keeps it simple - just tap clusters of matching tiles and watch them disappear. This creates a different rhythm compared to traditional match-3 games. Levels move at a nice clip and there's something really satisfying about watching those colorful explosions when you clear a big group.
